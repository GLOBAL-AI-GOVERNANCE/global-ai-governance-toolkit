# v2.5.0 — Organizational AI Maturity Evidence

Global AI Governance Toolkit v2.5.0 publishes organizational AI maturity evidence.

## Shipped

- Organizational AI maturity evidence profile. A human determines the maturity level. The evaluator checks whether submitted evidence can carry that determination.
- Four level labels only: Basic, Ready, Dynamic, and Advanced. No numerical score.
- Partial and full-framework coverage. Partial coverage is not framework completion.
- A local-only browser for assessment, review, and export of `profile-result.json`.
- Machine-interface version 1.2.0.

## Boundaries

- `authority_effect` remains NONE.
- No certification, compliance determination, deployment authorization, or authority transfer.
- No independent machine declaration of organizational maturity.
- Framework attribution stays with the isolated adapter. Use does not imply publisher endorsement.
