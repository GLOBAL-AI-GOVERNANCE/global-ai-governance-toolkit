# CISO AI Risk & Accountability Profile v0.1

This optional experimental profile turns a bounded, referenced AI governance decision into a decision-first profile result. It keeps the primary Decision Pack as the public governance product and preserves accountable human authority.

The result highlights current decision, accountable roles, top condition, stop condition, evidence state, review expiration, and dependency state. It does not authorize deployment, accept risk, certify compliance, establish safety, or make a legal determination.
