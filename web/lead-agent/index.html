How the guard decides

One order, every call.

  1. Level 5 is denied. Spending money or deleting records is never an agent action here, whoever asks.
  2. Passport and lineage. The agent and every parent above it must be active. Suspending or revoking a parent stops all its descendants.
  3. Inventory and ceiling. The tool must be in the agent's passport, and its Action Level at or under the passport ceiling.
  4. Agent Fleet Controls. Spawning, network calls and spending must be granted to this agent, and no spawn may exceed delegation depth 3.
  5. Level 4 approver. The operator's automated approver clears listed Level 4 actions. Anything else at Level 4 waits for a person.
  6. Levels 1 to 3 run unattended. Every decision, result and control lands in the audit ledger.

What this is, and is not.

A reference implementation and a demonstration. In this tab, passports are simulated and unsigned and tools are simulated; nothing leaves the page. The local server issues signed passports and validates them with the published tools. Results are non-authorizing (authority_effect: NONE): they do not certify an agent, grant permission, or replace an accountable person.
