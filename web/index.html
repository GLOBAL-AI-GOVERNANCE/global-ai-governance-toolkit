AI governance pre-flight review

Check your AI systems and agents before they act.

List each AI system or agent in a simple inventory. The toolkit shows which ones are missing a named owner, monitoring, a shutdown path, or evidence, and prepares a Decision Pack so an accountable human can make and document the decision. It runs in this browser: no install, no upload, no login.
