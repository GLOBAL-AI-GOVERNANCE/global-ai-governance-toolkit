For anyone who uses, builds, deploys, oversees, researches, or regulates AI. A few examples follow; every step is open, so begin anywhere.

  • You use AI appsChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini, or Copilot. Check the AI you use and give it a ground rule, in your browser.

The suite

  • Toolkit

    Turn an AI-system inventory into findings and a Decision Pack for the person accountable.

    v2.8.2 · stable

    The same checks, callable by your agents over Model Context Protocol (MCP). Read-only.

    v0.5.1 · released

    v0.1.0-alpha.5 · available

    Track each vulnerability from owner to fix to retest, and close it only with evidence reviewed by a separate role.

    v0.4.0 · released

  • AI Cyber Resilience Framework

    Limit the blast radius of AI-accelerated cyber risk, protect recovery, and keep critical work running.

    v0.1.1 · released

  • Containment Ledger

    Review a system's security boundaries in your browser: domains, paths, evidence, and a human disposition.

    v1.0.0 · released

  • Peace OS: Crisis Room

    v0.3.0 · available

  • Governed Systems Administration

    Contracts for human-governed, AI-assisted systems administration, specified ahead of any execution.

    Specification · review open

  • AI Governance Foundations

    The governance model, registers, and templates the rest of the suite builds on.

    v1.1.1 · stable

Toolkit · v2.8.2

Run a check in this browser.

List each AI system or agent in a simple inventory. The Toolkit shows which ones lack a named owner, monitoring, a shutdown path, or evidence, and prepares a Decision Pack so the person accountable can make and record the decision. No install, no upload, no login.

Advisory vs enforcement. Browser and CLI results are advisory. Runtime enforcement is the MCP guard and hooks.

Scope

      
Where the tools stop

      

        
The decision stays human
Every output carries authority_effect: NONE. Approval, deployment, risk acceptance, and shutdown remain with an accountable person.

        
Checks are bounded
Automated checks support human review. They do not approve deployment, certify compliance, or accept risk. Framework crosswalks are informative and establish no compliance. Each product's status is stated as it stands today.

        
Independent
Compatible with Model Context Protocol (MCP) based agents. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any AI lab or platform.
