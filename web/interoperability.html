How to read a row

  • TESTED — we ran the path against the published open-source tool or config (usually with a mock model).
  • AVAILABLE — a recipe, adapter, or file ships; launch or offline coverage only.
  • DOCUMENTED — research notes; no adapter yet, or an honest gap.

Our runs are TESTED, never VERIFIED. Product use does not wait on external reviews.

Lead Agent

The browser Lead Agent runs a deterministic mock model with no account or key. Optional local serve from Agentic AI Governance talks to OpenAI-, Anthropic-, or xAI-compatible endpoints when keys sit in the local environment. Keys never enter the browser.

Limitations

  • Adapters wrap Action Levels and passports; they do not authorize any action.
  • Client-inside sessions are mostly launch-tested, not session-tested.
  • Evidence gates that wait on outside parties stay unchanged and are not required for these paths.
